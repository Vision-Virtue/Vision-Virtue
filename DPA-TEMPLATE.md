## 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this DPA, the terms "**Personal Data**", "**Data Subject**", "**Processing**", "**Controller**", "**Processor**", "**Sub-Processor**", and "**Supervisory Authority**" have the meanings given in the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law 5741-1981 ("**PPL**") and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("**GDPR**").

"**Service**" means the Visibility Portal as described in the Terms of Service at visionvirtuepartnership.com/terms.html.

"**Customer Data**" means all data, including Personal Data, that Customer or its authorised users submit to the Service or that is generated by the Service on Customer's behalf.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

3.1 Customer is the **Controller** of all Personal Data submitted to or generated by the Service.

3.2 Vision & Virtue is the **Processor** and shall:
   (a) process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer, including the instructions embedded in the configured Service features;
   (b) not process Personal Data for any purpose other than providing the Service to Customer;
   (c) ensure that persons authorised to process Personal Data are committed to confidentiality;
   (d) implement the technical and organisational measures set out in **Annex A** below;
   (e) assist Customer in responding to Data Subject requests under Section 6 below;
   (f) notify Customer of personal data breaches per Section 7 below;
   (g) at termination, delete or return all Personal Data per Section 8 below;
   (h) make available to Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

## 5. Security measures

## 6. Data Subject rights

6.1 Vision & Virtue shall, taking into account the nature of the Processing, assist Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as possible, to fulfil Customer's obligation to respond to requests by Data Subjects exercising their rights under PPL / GDPR.

6.2 Specifically, Vision & Virtue shall:
   (a) within 5 business days of Customer's written request, provide an export of all Personal Data relating to a specific Data Subject in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format;
   (b) within 5 business days of Customer's written request, irreversibly delete all Personal Data relating to a specific Data Subject (subject to legal retention exemptions, if any).

6.3 If Vision & Virtue receives a request from a Data Subject directly, it shall forward the request to Customer within 3 business days and shall not respond to the Data Subject unless legally required or expressly authorised by Customer.

---

## 7. Personal data breaches

7.1 Vision & Virtue shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within **72 hours** of becoming aware, of any Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Data.

7.2 The notification shall include, where available:
   (a) a description of the nature of the breach including categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;
   (b) the likely consequences of the breach;
   (c) measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its possible adverse effects;
   (d) the name and contact details of Vision & Virtue's data protection contact.

7.3 Vision & Virtue shall cooperate with Customer and provide all assistance reasonably necessary for Customer to comply with its own notification obligations to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority and / or affected Data Subjects under applicable law.

---

## 8. Return or deletion of Personal Data

8.1 On termination of the Service, or earlier upon Customer's written request, Vision & Virtue shall, at Customer's option:
   (a) return all Personal Data to Customer in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format; or
   (b) irreversibly delete all Personal Data, including from backups (with backup deletion completing within the standard 30-day backup retention window).

8.2 Vision & Virtue shall confirm such deletion in writing on Customer's request.

8.3 Vision & Virtue may retain Personal Data to the extent required by applicable law, provided that such Personal Data remains protected by the security measures of this DPA for as long as it is retained.

   (b) responses to Customer's reasonable written questionnaires regarding security and privacy practices.

9.2 If the above are insufficient to demonstrate compliance, Customer (or a mutually agreed third-party auditor bound by confidentiality) may conduct an on-site audit at Vision & Virtue's offices on 30 days' notice during normal business hours, at Customer's expense.

# ANNEX A — Technical and Organisational Security Measures

Vision & Virtue maintains the following measures as of the effective date of this DPA. Measures may be updated from time to time, provided the level of security does not materially decrease.

## A.1 Pseudonymisation and encryption

| Control | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Encryption in transit | TLS 1.2+ for all HTTPS traffic between browsers, the Vision & Virtue backend, and all sub-processors. |
| Encryption at rest — application layer | AES-256-GCM column-level encryption applied to sensitive fields in the Visibility Portal database: employee names, monthly salaries, salary allocations, budget descriptions, vendor names, and any field containing free-text customer narrative. |
| Encryption at rest — infrastructure layer | Render persistent disk is encrypted by the hosting provider. |
| Encryption key management | Encryption keys are stored exclusively as Render environment variables (`DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, `BACKUP_ENCRYPTION_KEY`). Keys are generated using a cryptographically secure random source (256 bits each). Keys are never committed to source control, included in logs, or shared in chat. |
| Customer access key storage | Customer access keys (VV-XXXXXX) are stored as scrypt hashes (N=16384, r=8, p=1) with a 16-byte per-row random salt. The original key cannot be recovered from the database. |

## A.2 Ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, resilience

| Control | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Access control — V&V personnel | Only personnel with a specific business need have access to production data. Access is granted via individual accounts, never shared credentials. |
| Authentication — Customers | Per-customer access key. Failed authentication attempts are rate-limited per IP (5 per 15 minutes) and per key (5 consecutive failures triggers a 1-hour lockout on that key). |
| Logical isolation | All Visibility Portal tables include a `customer_key_id` foreign key with `ON DELETE CASCADE`. Every API endpoint resolves the requesting customer's key and queries are scoped to that customer's data only. |
| AI Agent scoping | Every call to the AI CFO Agent includes the requesting `customer_key_id`. The application layer rejects any context containing data linked to a different customer. AI responses are scanned for tokens that suggest cross-tenant data leakage before being returned. |
| AI Agent rate limiting | Each customer is limited to 100 AI Agent queries per hour to limit the rate of any potential data exfiltration via Q&A. |
| Audit logging | Every AI Agent prompt and response is hashed (SHA-256) and logged with timestamp, customer key id, agent type, and any leak-detection flags. Logs retained for 90 days. |
| Backup | Daily automatic backups by Render. Additional daily off-site encrypted backups retained 30 days. |
| Disaster recovery | RPO 24 hours, RTO 24 hours. |

## A.3 Restoration after physical / technical incident

Backups are tested for restorability at least quarterly. Recovery procedures are documented in the Vision & Virtue Incident Response Playbook.

## A.4 Regular testing of effectiveness

| Control | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Vulnerability scanning | Dependencies scanned weekly via `npm audit`. Critical / high CVEs patched within 7 days. |
| Code review | All changes to the Visibility Portal codebase reviewed before deployment. |
| Penetration testing | At Customer's reasonable request and at intervals no more frequent than annually, Vision & Virtue will arrange a third-party penetration test at Vision & Virtue's expense. |

## A.5 Personnel

| Control | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Confidentiality | All Vision & Virtue personnel and engaged contractors sign written confidentiality undertakings. |
| Training | Annual data protection awareness training. |
| Termination | On termination of engagement, access to all systems revoked within 24 hours. |
