            How CF Structure works

            Opening Balances (O.B): the closing balance from your prior year. Liabilities (A/P, accrued salaries) are credit balances, entered as a positive amount and displayed as ($X,XXX). Assets (cash, A/R, inventory) are debit balances, displayed as $X,XXX.

            Payment terms (Payables / Receivables): each row picks a term — Cash, 30+, 60+, 90+, 120+, 180+. The cash-out (or cash-in) month = expense month + lag-in-days. For example, a 60+ expense in March is paid in May.

            Prior-period carry: yellow "Enter" cells in early months for terms longer than the budget can look back. Empty = $0 — no obligation unless you actually had pre-year activity to settle.

            Finalize unlocks once Opening Cash + every required section badge says Filled. Optional sections (Other Adjustments / Financing / Capex) don't gate it.

            Inventory summary

            Purchases are free-input per month. COGS is auto-pulled from the budget — sum of all lines whose Budget Category is Finished goods under COGS.

            3 Salaries & Benefits Required

            Accrued salary liability from the prior year. Enter a positive amount; displayed as ($X,XXX) — credit balance.

            Salaries summary

            Expenses are auto-pulled from the budget — sum of all lines whose Budget Category is Salaries and benefits. Payments are fixed at 30+ arrears (Jan is the only free input; Feb–Dec auto-equal the previous month's Expenses).

            4 Other Adjustments Optional

            Free-form adjustments per period. Enter positive amounts as 1,234 and negative as (1,234). FY column auto-sums the 12 months.

            5 Financing Optional

            Typical rows: Interest expense, Interest on deposits, Fundraising. Cash inflows positive; outflows in (parens).

            6 Capex Optional

            Capital expenditures (cash outflows) — e.g. Laptops, Office build-out, Lab equipment. Enter as (parens).
